About Us

Family Owned Since 1999.

Wingz It Iz was built on a simple promise: fresh food, bold flavor, generous portions, and prices that make sense.

Our Story

Fresh Food Made with Care.

Wingz It Iz is a family-owned restaurant proudly serving Chicagoland with fresh, made-to-order meals and welcoming service. Our menu brings together signature Whole Wingz, Party Wingz, seafood, sandwiches, and our fresh Rice Bowls available at our Hickory Hills location.

We use quality ingredients, house-made sauces, and bold seasonings to create meals that are flavorful, filling, and worth coming back for.

Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch, bringing dinner home to your family, or feeding a celebration, our goal is to provide dependable quality and exceptional service every single time.
