Frequently Asked Questions

About Wingz It Iz

Find quick answers about our locations, menu, ordering, and catering.

What is Wingz It Iz known for?

Wingz It Iz is known as the Home of the Famous Garlic Wingz, with fresh made-to-order whole wingz, party wingz, seafood, sandwiches, fries, and family meals.

Where are Wingz It Iz restaurants located?

Wingz It Iz has three Chicagoland locations in Chicago, Alsip, and Hickory Hills, Illinois.

Does Wingz It Iz offer catering?

Yes. Wingz It Iz accepts catering and large food orders for parties, offices, game days, family gatherings, school events, and other special occasions.

Which Wingz It Iz location serves rice bowls?

Fresh rice bowls are available at the Hickory Hills location.

Is Wingz It Iz food made to order?

Yes. Wingz It Iz prepares meals fresh to order with bold seasonings, sauces, and generous portions.

How can I place an order?

Choose your nearest location and call ahead for pickup. Phone numbers and current hours are available on the Hours and Locations page.
